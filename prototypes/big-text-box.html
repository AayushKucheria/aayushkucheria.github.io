about

I care about AI being good for the people it touches. Two questions hold most of my attention:

1 What's in the latent space, and can we learn to read it and reach in?

2 What happens where a human and an AI meet, and how do we design that interface?

Both questions point to something far more complex, subtle, and fertile than first appears, rewarding a kind of close attention I'm drawn towards.
