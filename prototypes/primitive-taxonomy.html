Corner + Label-list
The "where" — decision-free. A corner is one cloth (one tab). A label-list is a stitch-label with a flow of pieces beneath it.
Authoring a corner reads like this — no coordinates anywhere:

corner "work" → stitch-label → drop patch, patch, patch…
corner "reading" → stitch-label "machines & minds" → spine, spine, spine
The whole idea in one line: you decide what exists (a patch, a photo, a mark) and which corner it lives in. The flow and the tilt are automatic. The look stays handmade because the pieces and the weave carry it — not because you hand-placed anything.
