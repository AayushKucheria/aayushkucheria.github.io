# Personal Website — Spec

Status: planning. Drafted with Aayush in one session; resume in a fresh chat.
See "Open / next session" at the bottom.

## Who it's for
Cold strangers evaluating "work-Aayush" — people who land with no prior context
and form a *feeling* within ~90 seconds. Not friends, not a recruiter with a
checklist.

## What they should walk away feeling
- Expansion, and a clear design sensibility. The site feels *cared for* —
  spacious, considered, no cruft.
- An understanding of the synthesis (below); a sense their interests intersect
  his.
- A pull: want to follow him, talk to him, work with him.
- They know how to reach him.

## The spine  (CONFIRMED)
Interface design and AI safety are not two interests to balance — they are one
care at two scales. The question underneath both: *do our tools expand what a
mind can think and do, or quietly narrow it?* A bad interface bureaucratizes one
person's thought; a misaligned model bureaucratizes a civilization's. Aayush
works the same problem at both ends.

The site is a **demonstration of that care**, not a catalogue of work. It is
itself an interface, and is to be judged as one — Aayush confirmed he wants that
bar.

## Non-goals
- Not a CV / résumé dump.
- Not a comprehensive listing of all 29 repos. Curated only.
- Not a blog. Not "currently reading" / status signaling.

## How the work proceeds  (CONFIRMED)
In milestones. Each milestone is complete in itself — a site Aayush is satisfied
to have live — and he advances to the next when he wants to.

## Milestone 1 — The Home  (scope CONFIRMED — option A)
A single complete page — a complete one-page site that *also shows the work* —
replacing the current Chirpy site entirely. In one scroll:

1. Opening — name + a spine line. The 90-second feeling begins here.
2. The synthesis — stated short, in Aayush's voice.
3. Work as instances of the care — ~5 curated highlights, brief, framed as
   "the care in action," not a CV.
4. Orientation — a light line on the current role (Research Fellow, Live
   Machinery).
5. Open door — email, GitHub, LinkedIn, X.

One page, one scroll, no navigation.
